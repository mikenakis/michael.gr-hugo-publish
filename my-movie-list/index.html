    My Movie List

    2026-07-21 Tue 17:06:04 CEST
    46 minute read

    This is a list of movies that I have watched, with a short remark for each.

    It also includes some movies that I have not watched yet, but I plan to watch.

    The "Status" column is one of the following:

    • The date when I watched the movie, (although I may have also watched the movie at an unknown earlier date,) or
    • "(?)" if I watched the movie before mid-2015, when I began keeping track, or if I forgot to record the date at the time of watching it.
    • "Not yet" if I intend to watch the movie but have not watched it yet.

    The "Rank" column is one of the following:

    • 💛 Personal favorite. Usually, (but not always,) a must-watch.
    • ⭐ Must-watch.
    • 👍 OK to watch.
    • 🤷 Meh.
    • 👎 Do not watch.
    • 🎦 Not sure / undecided / not watched.

    Some of those movies I watched a very, very long time ago, possibly even on Greek Public TV in the 1980s, so some of the remarks reflect the impression that a much younger me was left with after watching a movie.
