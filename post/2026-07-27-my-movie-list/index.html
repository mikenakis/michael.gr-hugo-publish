    2026-07-21 Tue 17:06:04 CEST

    This is a list of movies that I have watched, with a short remark for each. It also includes some movies I plan to watch.

    The "Watched" column is one of the following:

    • The date when I watched the movie, or
    • "(?)" if I watched the movie before mid-2015, when I began keeping track, or if I forgot to record the date at the time of watching it.
    • "Not yet" if I intend to watch the movie but have not watched it yet.

    Some of those movies I watched a very, very long time ago, possibly even on Greek Public TV in the 1980s, so the remark reflects the impression that a much younger me was left with after watching the movie.
